01 — About

Warm support, thoughtfully organised.

AG was created from a simple idea: sometimes families do not need a traditional tour guide, a full-time employee or a travel agency — they simply need a reliable person who can help with the details.

Hello & welcome

Hello and welcome to AG Services. My name is Glory, and I am a nursing student with a warm heart, a happy soul, and a genuine love for people.

I enjoy children, learning, creativity, adventure, travelling, discovering new places and creating beautiful memories. I especially enjoy helping families feel comfortable, supported and organised.

My nursing background has taught me the importance of responsibility, patience, cleanliness, observation, empathy and caring for others. Beyond my studies, I am naturally curious and adventurous — discovering places, trying new activities, taking pictures, learning about different cultures and turning ordinary moments into memorable experiences.

This is what inspired me to create AG Services.

“You enjoy the moment. We help take care of the details.”

Whether you need help with your children, your home, your daily schedule, your holiday activities or English lessons, AG offers flexible support according to your needs.

05 — Important

A clear, transparent service.

AG is a personal support and accompaniment service. Clear boundaries help keep expectations safe and professional.

AG is not

  • A travel agency
  • A professional tour-guide service
  • A transportation company
  • A medical service
  • An activity provider

For third-party activities

  • For surfing, swimming, horse riding, boat trips and similar activities, the appropriate professional provider is responsible for the activity itself, including safety rules and age requirements.
  • Transportation, entrance tickets, meals and third-party activity fees are not included unless specifically agreed upon in advance.
  • Services are discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

A simple mission 🌸

Where parents can enjoy their time. Where children can play, learn and explore. Where visitors can discover Morocco without feeling overwhelmed. And where everyday tasks become a little easier.
